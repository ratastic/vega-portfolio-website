## community quiz
### description
my quiz centers on traditional hong kong dishes. The user is presented with 8 questions that ask for food and beverage preferences, which then generate a recommendation based on the answers. the former 6 questions are about food; the latter 2 questions are about drink. this quiz utilizes local storage and event listeners. 

the goal of this quiz was to bridge food lovers and my culture. i would love for people to experience the culture I grew up with. 

### functionality
navigating the quiz is fairly simple. the first page asks for a name, which is used to produce a personalized recommendation at the end. the second page houses all the food and drink questions; to ensure the user is aware of their inputs, the answer buttons highlight upon clicking. the final page displays the recommendation and provides images and descritions for both items.

## divination experience
### description
this projects focuses on reincarnation, revealing which insect the user was in their past life and in their next life through tarot card-esque interactions. this experience utilizes the iNaturalist insect API, of which i sourced the listed colloquial insect names and their corresponding photographs. 

i am very fond of insects; i wanted to create an experience that allowed them to be seen in a positive light. 

### functionality
the experience is comprised of three parts. the first part presents a tarot card triplet, and, upon clicking one, a randomly selected insect from the API is displayed; this represents the insect you were in your past life. the second part houses the same format, except this represents the insect you will be in your next life. the final part shows the user their results side-by-side along with photographs.
